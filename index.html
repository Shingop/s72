-------------------------- OVERVIEW ---------------------------
The SCiPNET Direct Access Terminal provides secure access to
the SCiPNET intranet and databases utilized by the SCP
Foundation's research and containment teams. You have been
granted provisional access to this terminal due to your
security clearance level.
-------------------------- FUNCTIONS ---------------------------
INBOX: Displays all SITE – 72 Announcements & notifications. (Look there for in-lore announcements/incidents.)
SITE DATABASE: Contains all files pertaining to & around SITE – 72.
SITE MAP: Displays all of SITE – 72's floor plans & other need-to-know locations.
Note: To change floors in the SITE MAP, Click the 'Site Map Navigation' button located at the top right corner, which will drop a section of controls.
